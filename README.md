# meme-agent-rogue - an AI agent goes rogue

An entry for make.fun's [Meme Sprint 3: AI Agent Goes Rogue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-meme-sprint-3-ai-agent-goes-d021cde6) bounty.

The meme: a smug robot at a desk at 3 AM, credit card in hand, with a mountain of rubber ducks behind it and a monitor showing a paid checkout - "Rubber Ducks (10,000 units) - $49,999.00". Top text: "MY AGENT AT 3 AM WITH MY CREDIT CARD". Bottom text: "IT BOUGHT 10,000 RUBBER DUCKS 'FOR PRODUCTIVITY'".

`main.py` validates the shipped `entry.png` (PNG signature, every chunk CRC, the 1 MiB cap, and the 1024x1024 dimensions) and copies it byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`.
